# drone/: HoverAir X1

Everything for the **HoverAir X1** (Zero Zero Robotics): the small foldable white drone with
caged propellers. Code that talks to the drone, its adapters and its notes live here.
Board label: `area: drone`.

## What we know
- Palm-sized, foldable, propeller guards; normally flown from the HOVER X1 phone app.
- Comes with a charging case/battery pack (in the kit photo next to the Bobac).

Add findings here as you confirm them.
