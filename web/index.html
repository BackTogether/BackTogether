Three steps

  1. 1

    Send the photo

    A normal phone photo where the face is visible. It is downscaled in your browser and sent straight to the search team's robot.

  2. 2

    The drone launches

    The Bobac3 base wakes the folded drone, the arms unfold, the rotors spin up and it lifts off the deck on its own.

  3. 3

    The zone is scanned

    The drone flies a search grid over the area you named, matching faces from the air. Event staff get the hit and bring your child to you.
